# Practical ideas for growing reach and followers on X

## Comment Candidates

Periodically search for relevant posts where a specific, useful reply could add to the conversation. Save promising posts in a persistent **Comment Candidates** section with the post author, text, link, relevance reason, and an AI recommended comment. Offer a few alternative drafts and let the user edit, approve, or dismiss each candidate. Never publish replies without explicit approval. Rotate through target topics, remove duplicates, and avoid repeatedly resurfacing posts that were already handled.

## Consistent original posts

Use the existing Post Ideas and research features to build a small weekly queue. Track which drafts were published, and vary the angle so posts do not repeat the same point.

## Personalized writing feedback

Use approved, edited, and dismissed recommendations to learn the user's preferred voice and the kinds of replies they consider useful. Apply that feedback to later comment drafts.

## Lightweight growth review

Summarize which topics, post formats, and conversations led to useful engagement or new followers. Use the results to shape future post ideas and candidate searches.

## Working principles

- Prefer timely, relevant conversations where the user can contribute a concrete observation or answer.
- Make each reply specific to the source post; avoid generic agreement and copied comments.
- Keep discovery periodic and configurable. If the app runs locally, scheduled discovery can only run while the app is open.
- Keep a person in the loop before publishing. X disallows repeated, duplicated, and unsolicited replies.
